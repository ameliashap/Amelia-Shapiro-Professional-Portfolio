Two classes shaping my first year at Tulane, and an AI literacy minor I would like to pursue.

I / CURRENT COURSEWORK

Comparative politics

My comparative politics class is a starting point for exploring how governments differ, how institutions shape participation, and why political outcomes vary across countries.

A question I'm exploring

Why do similar political institutions produce different outcomes across countries?

II / CURRENT COURSEWORK

International relations

In my international relations class, I'm interested in how states interact through diplomacy, cooperation, and conflict, and how decisions in one country affect others.

A question I'm exploring

What makes countries cooperate when their interests do not fully align?

III / MINOR INTEREST

AI literacy

I'd like to minor in AI literacy to better understand how AI works, where its limitations lie, and what responsible use means for education and public policy.

A question I'm exploring

How should governments balance the benefits of AI with questions of bias, accountability, and public trust?
